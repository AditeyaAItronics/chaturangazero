How this variant works
  • There is no check. Any move is legal, even leaving your Raja attacked.
  • Capture the Raja to win. The game ends only when a Raja is actually taken.
  • Stalemate wins for the player who cannot move.
  • Bare Raja loses (Raja against Raja is a draw).
  • Gaja jumps exactly two squares diagonally, over anything.
  • Mantri moves one square diagonally. Padati moves one step, no double step, and always promotes to Mantri.
  • No castling, no en passant, no repetition or 50-move rule; games stop at 300 plies.
